## Residual risk
Follower listeners wait on the lead's flock with no deadline, so a hung signal-cli send in the lead holds every finished session until it returns. The concurrent read-modify-write in FileGathering.update can lose a `gone` or `sent` field, which mislists a session but does not cross replies.
