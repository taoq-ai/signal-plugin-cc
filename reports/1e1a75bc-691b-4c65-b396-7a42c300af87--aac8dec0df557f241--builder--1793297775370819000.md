**What changed**
- **Digest:** each finished session's listener records itself in a shared list. The first one to take a lock waits 10 seconds. If two or more sessions have not sent yet, it sends one digest and a "Which session?" poll; otherwise it sends today's single message.
- **Port and adapter:** the lock is in a new `Gathering` port, implemented by `FileGathering` in `signal_cc/adapters/files.py`. It keeps one file per session under `gather/` and the lock on `gather.lock`. Only `signal_cc/entry/wiring.py` builds it.
- **Routing** (`signal_cc/domain/routing.py`):
  - A quote or reaction on the digest or its poll gets the hint from the oldest session still waiting.
  - A poll vote makes only the picked session send its full message; nothing wakes.
  - A tag of a session taken over at the Mac gets "[tag] That session no longer listens here; answer at the Mac." Those tags stay known until the next digest starts.
- **Merge:** #8 had landed, so I merged `origin/main` without rebasing. The only conflict was one import line in `tests/test_adapters.py`. The gather files now follow #8's rules: a 0700 folder, 0600 files, and no writes through a symlink. Two new tests cover this.
- **Poll size:** the signal-cli 0.14.9 man page allows 2 to 10 options of 1 to 100 characters. The poll holds the first 10 sessions; every session stays reachable by its tag. If the poll is refused, the text digest still goes out.
