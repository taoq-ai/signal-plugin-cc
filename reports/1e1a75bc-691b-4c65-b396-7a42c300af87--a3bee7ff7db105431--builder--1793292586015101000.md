**What changed**

**Assumptions** (also in the PR body)
- Files already in the owner's home stay 0644. Once the home is 0700 other accounts cannot reach them, and changing each file's mode could reach a file outside the home through a hard link.
- If the home is itself a symlink, its target gets tightened. A symlink inside the home is refused.
- I read "no output" in the acceptance as stdout. When relay.log is a symlink, the hook still writes the existing one-line journal error to stderr and exits 0.
